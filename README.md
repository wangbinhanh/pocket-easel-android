# Pocket Easel for Android

Quick color studies, wherever you are. A gamut mask decides which colors are on your palette, and a simple gesture lets you mix as you paint — a little warmer, a little cooler, lighter, darker — without leaving the picture.

This page is for downloading Pocket Easel **directly as an APK**, for phones without Google Play. If your phone has Google Play, you can get it from the Play Store instead.

## Download

**[⬇︎ Download the latest version](https://github.com/wangbinhanh/pocket-easel-android/releases/latest)**

On that page, tap **PocketEasel-x.x.apk** under *Assets*.

Needs Android 8.0 or newer.

## Install

1. Open the downloaded file (from your browser's downloads, or the notification).
2. If your phone says it can't install apps from this source, tap **Settings**, turn on **Allow from this source**, then go back and tap **Install**.
3. Open Pocket Easel from your home screen.

Some phones add one more step:

- **Samsung**: if installation is blocked by *Auto Blocker*, go to Settings › Security and privacy › Auto Blocker, turn it off, install, then turn it back on.
- **Honor / Huawei**: if *Pure mode* blocks it, search "Pure mode" in Settings and turn it off for the install.
- **Xiaomi / Redmi / POCO**: tap through the security scan and choose **Install anyway**.

## Update

Download the new version and install it **over the old one**. Your paintings stay.

⚠️ **Don't uninstall first.** Paintings are stored on your phone, so uninstalling deletes them.

The app doesn't check for updates by itself. To hear about new versions, tap **Watch › Custom › Releases** at the top of this page (needs a GitHub account), or follow [@wangbin_art](https://www.instagram.com/wangbin_art/) on Instagram.

## Free and Pro

All painting tools and image export are free. Exported images come with a frame and a small *Pocket Easel* watermark.

**Pocket Easel Pro** is a one-time purchase (US$5.99) that lets you:

- turn off the frame,
- remove the watermark, or replace it with your own name,
- export time-lapse videos of your painting.

You can try the Pro settings in the export panel before buying.

### Unlock Pro

1. In Pocket Easel, open **Export** and tap **Unlock**.
2. Tap **Buy Pro on Gumroad** and complete the purchase. You'll get a **license key** on the receipt page and by email.
3. Back in the app, paste the key and tap **Activate**.

Activation needs internet once. After that, Pro works offline. You can activate the same key on your other phones.

The Gumroad license only works in this APK version. The Google Play version is a separate app with its own purchase.

## Privacy

Pocket Easel has no account, no ads and no analytics. Your paintings never leave your phone unless you share them. The only time the app goes online is to check your license key with Gumroad. Full [privacy policy](https://cumbersome-belief-de6.notion.site/Pocket-Easel-Privacy-Policy-Android-APK-version-7354bd5b89308293a8bf819d85df250d).
